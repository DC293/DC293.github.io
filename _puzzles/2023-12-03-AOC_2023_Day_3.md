## Intro
The [Advent of Code](https://adventofcode.com/2023) is a yearly series of puzzles run during the month of December. The daily puzzles are the same for all players but puzzle inputs are randomly generated for each user account (to stop cheating!). The solutions outlined in this blog series are designed to intake any puzzle input and output the correct answer. 


## Part 1
We are given a puzzle containing lines of periods, symbols and numbers. For the first task, we must identify numbers touching symbols, including those in the lines above and below, and on the diagonal. This is best understood when visualised. In the example puzzle section, we would identify:
  - 26 (Line 1)
  - 315 (Line 2)
  - 66 (Line 5)
  - 4 (Line 6)
  - 7 (Line 7)
Numbers 8 (Line 4) and 35 (Line 7) are ignored as they are nto adjacent to any symbols. Our answer is the sum of all the numbers touching symbols.
